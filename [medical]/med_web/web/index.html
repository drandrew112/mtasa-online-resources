What we do

One service, four parts working together from the first call to the hospital bed.

🚑

Emergency response

Ambulance crews at BLS and ALS level, doctor units, solo responders and an air ambulance. Each crew carries its own bag and monitor and is trained for CPR, airway management, IV access and splinting.

📡

Dispatch centre

Every call becomes a task in the Emergency Response Manager. Dispatchers set its priority from P1 to P4 and send the nearest free unit, which receives the case on its in-vehicle tablet.

🏥

Hospital network

Our crews hand their patients over in the ambulance bays of the state's hospitals. The route to the nearest one is set automatically when a crew leaves the scene.

🎓

Training

New paramedics start with a guided tutorial on the tablet, the equipment and the procedures, and learn on real calls next to experienced colleagues.

How we prioritise

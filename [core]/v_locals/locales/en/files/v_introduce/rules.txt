FreeV – Server Rules

By playing on the server, you agree to follow the rules listed below. Violating these rules may result in a warning, mute, admin jail, or ban, depending on the severity of the violation. Administrators make decisions on a case-by-case basis and, whenever possible, carefully review all sides of the situation.

1. Respect
- Treat other players with respect. Insults, harassment, threats, racism, sexism, and any form of hate speech are prohibited – whether in chat, private messages, crew names, or anywhere else.
- Discrimination is prohibited. Attacking ethnic or sexual minorities is not allowed. Violations of this rule may result in severe consequences.
- Do not spam the chat or advertise other servers or services.
- Do not impersonate an administrator.

2. Fair Play
- Cheating, hacks, trainers, macros, or any modifications that give you an unfair advantage over other players are prohibited.
- Do not exploit bugs or glitches. If you discover one, report it to an administrator.
- Do not use multiple accounts to gain an advantage (money, XP, items).

3. Killing and Violence
- Do not kill other players without a valid reason.
- Do not unnecessarily obstruct other players' movement. Exception: police pursuits or arrest warrants.
- Do not attack AFK players, players in the tutorial, or players who are using menus. A small tablet icon next to a player's nametag indicates when they are currently using a menu or panel.
- Do not repeatedly kill players immediately after they respawn.
- These rules do not apply to activities with their own specific rules (races, deathmatches, Arena War).

4. Jobs and Emergency Services
- Players who are on duty in emergency services (e.g. the Ambulance Service) must not be targeted. Let them do their jobs.
- Work vehicles and equipment must only be used for their intended work purposes. Vehicles spawned through the interaction menu cannot be used for work purposes.
- Do not deliberately block hospitals, workstations, or workplaces.

5. Vehicles
- Do not ram, block, or damage other players' vehicles without a valid reason.
- Do not park in a way that blocks roads, entrances, or markers.

6. Money and Trading
- Do not scam other players. Agreements between players must be respected.
- Buying or selling in-game money, items, or accounts for real-world money is prohibited.

7. Names and Crews
- Player names, crew names, and crew tags must not be offensive or misleading.
- Follow the rules outlined in the "Respect" section when choosing your player name or crew name.

8. Administrators and Reports
- Before contacting an administrator, always check the F1 help panel. You may find answers to many of your questions there. Failure to do so may result in minor consequences.
- If someone violates the rules, use the Discord ticket system in the **create-ticket** channel. Describe what happened, who was involved, and when it happened. Make sure to attach evidence as well! Evidence is important, as without it, we cannot determine someone's guilt with complete certainty.
- Although Discord tickets are our primary reporting method, the **/report** command is also available in-game. However, assistance through this method is limited.
- Follow administrators' instructions. If you disagree with a decision, feel free to discuss it with the administrator or contact the server management team on Discord.
- Do not abuse the reporting system.

The rules are subject to change. If they are updated, you will be asked to read and accept them again.

Have fun playing!
The FreeV Team

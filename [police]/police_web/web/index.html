Become a police officer

Patrol the streets of Los Santos, stop vehicles, check papers, chase suspects and keep San Andreas safe. You are paid at the end of every shift for the reports you submitted.

How to join

  1. Go on duty at the LSPD station (Pershing Square, Los Santos): the duty marker is inside the building. Pick an outfit.
  2. Take a car in the underground garage of the station: Crown Victoria, Skoda Octavia or (from rank 2) a Police Ranger.
  3. Sign in to a unit on the Mobile Data Computer (J in the car): Adam (A-) with 2-4 officers or Lincoln (L-) alone.
  4. Set your status with F5 available, F6 responding, F7 busy, F4 pursuit.
  5. Work: stops (E hold on a pedestrian, Left Shift on the vehicle in front), radio (C), calls, pursuits.
  6. Submit your reports on the MDC and sign off: the pay comes for the submitted reports and the duty time.

What to expect

  • Traffic and pedestrian stops - identity checks, breathalyser and drug tests, warnings and fines from the penal code.
  • Pursuits - wanted suspects with AI units, road blocks and tactical vehicles at the highest levels.
  • Arrests - handcuffs, escort, the back seat of your car and the handover at the station.
  • Records - every citizen and vehicle in the police database, warrants, reports.
  • Services - backup from other units, ambulance and tow truck over the radio.

Ranks

Your rank grows with your experience in the force. From Second Lieutenant you may edit and revoke the warrants of others, from Captain you review and refund fines.

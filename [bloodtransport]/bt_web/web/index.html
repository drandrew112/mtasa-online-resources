Be the link between the blood bank and the ward.

Blood transporters carry blood products, lab samples and urgent medicine between the blood supply centers and the hospitals of San Andreas. Every unit counts – and every minute on the road matters.

What does a blood transporter do?

1

Pick up

Collect the prepared shipment at the counter of a blood bank or a hospital and put it in the transport box of your ambulance.

2

Plan

Several tasks can run at once and new ones arrive on the way. You choose the order and the route – urgent ones first, planned surgery blood in between.

3

Deliver

Hand the shipment over at the counter of the destination before it is too late. Lab samples travel the other way, from the hospital to the blood bank.

4

Cooperate

Overloaded? Hand a task that is still waiting for pickup over to a colleague who stands next to you.

How do I become a blood transporter?

STEP 1

Go to a blood transport duty marker (marked on your map) and go on duty. Duty gives you the transporter right.

STEP 2

Take a BT- ambulance at the vehicle point next to the duty marker.

STEP 3

Sit in it, press F6 and sign on. Leaving the service is possible anywhere.

STEP 4

Tasks arrive automatically. Take the box from the trunk, load it at the pickup counter, deliver it at the destination. Your pay is calculated when the service ends.

# Pursuits
@keywords: pursuit, chase, F4, join, AI police, road block, PIT, tank, wanted level
@related: wanted, police_units

## Starting and joining
`F4` (or the radio's Pursuit) starts a pursuit of the stopped vehicle or the vehicle in front of you; a player who does not stop gets the "failure to stop" wanted level. Every wanted player has a pursuit anyway; join it on the MDC **Pursuits** page. Units in a pursuit see the suspect on the radar (and the search circle while it is out of sight).

## AI police
The AI units join by the wanted level (one player unit = one AI car less):
- **1** – one car, follows, no shooting; on foot the officers walk up and arrest.
- **2** – two cars, they box the suspect in and cut it off on its predicted route; they shoot.
- **3** – three cars, PIT manoeuvres.
- **4** – four cars, ramming, road blocks ahead.
- **5–6** – tanks too.

AI cars appear and disappear only where no player looks. Their sirens change tone from time to time.

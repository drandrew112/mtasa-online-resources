# Going on duty
@keywords: duty, work, police work, outfit, uniform, police car, garage, rank, ranks
@related: police, police_units

## Duty
The duty point is inside the **LSPD** building (Pershing Square, Los Santos). Step into the marker and press `E`: pick an outfit and you are on duty, with the police right. Going off duty takes it away (and ends your unit).

## Vehicles
In the underground garage of the station:
- **Ford Crown Victoria** – the basic patrol car.
- **Skoda Octavia MK3** – police livery (hu_pd) and light bar; the MDC only works in the police livery.
- **Police Ranger** – off-road, from rank 2.

Every police car has emergency lights and siren (`0` lights, `1` siren, `2` next tone, `3` horn).

## Ranks
Your rank is your police work level; it shows on your name tag next to the work card. Rights by rank:
- every officer may issue warrants,
- from **Second Lieutenant** (rank 7) you may edit / revoke the warrants of others,
- from **Captain** (rank 9) you review and refund fines.

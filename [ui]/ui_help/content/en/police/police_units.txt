## Signing in
Units sign in **in a police car, on the MDC** (`J`). Until you are in a unit the MDC only shows the sign-in window.
- **Adam (A-)** – 2 to 4 officers. Invite the officers near you; they accept in a pop-up. An Adam unit never signs in alone.
- **Lincoln (L-)** – one officer.
- **Number** 1–99. A number is taken whatever the type (A-10 and L-10 cannot be on duty at the same time), on every server.

Officers can be added later too (Overview → Add officer). One officer is in one unit at a time.

## Status
The mini panel bottom right shows your callsign, the officers, the status and the keys:
- `F4` **Pursuit** (red) – starts a pursuit of the stopped vehicle / the vehicle in front
- `F5` **Available** (green)
- `F6` **Responding** (yellow)
- `F7` **Busy** (orange) – a stop adds the sub label (e.g. Traffic stop)

## Signing off
The leader signs off on the MDC; a member just leaves. **You cannot sign off while reports are waiting** – submit them first. If the leader leaves the server the unit ends (its pay comes once the reports are in); a member leaving does not end it.

# Inventory
@keywords: I, H, E, inventory, bag, item, hands, drop, trunk, storage, port
@related: binds

The inventory shows the items you carry (in your hands, on your back, in your bags). It cannot be opened in a vehicle.

## Keys

- `I` – open / close your own inventory
- `Esc` – close
- `H` – put the items in your hands down on the ground
- `E` – in a storage marker (for example at a trunk) open the storage
- Mouse wheel – scroll the left or right list (the one under the mouse)

## Moving items

- Left mouse button + drag – drag an item to another place or to the other side
- Hold `Shift` while dragging – move only one piece
- The arrow buttons next to the selected item move it to the other side quickly (with `Shift`: one piece)

# World: markers and 3D menus
@keywords: E, X, Q, marker, door, enter, shop, elevator, ferris wheel, 3D menu, interact, Y, HUD
@related: binds

## Markers

In most markers `E` is the use key. The screen always tells you when you stand in one.

- **Doors, buildings** – go in and out
- **Shops** – weapon shop (Ammu-Nation), clothes shop (Binco)
- **Work** – take or finish a work job
- **Elevator** – choose a floor
- **Organizations** – offices, sites
- **Aviation** – airport markers
- **Time trial** – start a run (as a driver)
- **Arena War** – the Arena War panel

## Ferris wheel

- `K` – get into the cabin
- `L` – get out

## 3D menus on objects

A small menu appears above some objects, vehicles and characters (for example a stretcher or a medical bag).

- `Q` / `E` – focus the previous / next nearby menu
- `X` – open / close the focused menu
- `1`–`9` (or the number pad) – pick a menu item
- `0` – next page, when the menu is longer
- `Backspace` – one level back / close

## HUD

- `Y` – shows the HUD info for a few seconds (money, level and XP)

# Social panel
@keywords: Home, Num 7, social, friend, friends, crew, message, messages
@related: binds, binds_phone, feature_social

On the social panel you manage your friends, your crew and your messages.

## Keys

- `Home` or `Num 7` – open / close
- `Esc` – close
- Mouse wheel – scroll the lists
- Mouse – click the buttons and text fields
- `Enter` – send / confirm in the active text field
- `Backspace` – delete in the active text field

## What can you do here?

- **Friends** – send and accept friend requests, your friend list.
- **Crew** – create a crew, members, invitations.
- **Messages** – private messages to other players.

> You can read your messages in the phone's Messages app too.

# Pause menu and map
@keywords: pause, P, menu, map, waypoint, gps, settings, stats, online, jobs, arena war
@related: binds, feature_pause

`P` opens the pause menu. It has four tabs: **Map**, **Online**, **Stats** and **Settings**.

## Using the menu

- `P` – open / close
- `Left` `Right` arrow – switch tabs
- `Enter` or `Down` arrow – enter the selected tab
- `Up` `Down` arrow – move in the list
- `Enter` – select, flip a toggle
- `Left` `Right` arrow – change a value in Settings (slider, choice)
- `Backspace` – one level back; on the tab row it closes the menu

## What can you do here?

- **Map** – the big map, route planning.
- **Online** – quick Job (race / deathmatch lobby), browse lobbies, official and community jobs, the Job Creator, Arena War, leave the server.
- **Stats** – level, XP and your statistics by category.
- **Settings** – graphics (draw distance, LOD, colour grading, …), model downloads, display, language, online options (own nametag, update e-mails).

## Map

The big map only opens from the pause menu (`F11` does nothing).

- Hold the left mouse button and drag – move the map
- `W` `A` `S` `D` – move the map
- Mouse wheel or `Num +` / `Num -` – zoom in / out
- `Space` – back to your own position
- Left click – place / remove a waypoint; the GPS plans the route
- `Enter` – waypoint at the centre of the map
- Arrows – move in the blip list, when there is one
- `Backspace` – close the map

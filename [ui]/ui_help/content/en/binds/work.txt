# Work jobs
@keywords: work, EMS, ambulance, tablet, J, blood transport, train driver, train, timetable, Sifa, F2, tutorial, stretcher, ATC
@related: binds, binds_world

Take a work job with `E` in its marker. You can have only one work job at a time.

## Tutorials

- `F2` – show / hide the cursor for the buttons of the tutorial card

## EMS

- `J` – open / close the EMS tablet (in an ambulance)
- Patients and equipment are handled with the 3D menus (`Q` / `E` / `X`, see the world page)
- Stretcher: right mouse button – cursor on / off, `Backspace` – cancel the selection

## Blood transport

- `J` – enlarge the panel in the bottom right corner (duty, tasks, handover)

## Train driver

- `Left Ctrl` – cursor on / off for the cab panel
- `F6` – open / close the timetable (scroll with the mouse wheel)
- `Space` – vigilance button (Sifa): press it now and then while moving and when a signal changes, or the train makes an emergency stop
- Hold the horn key – the train horn
- `F` – leave the cab (only from a standing train)

## Air traffic controller (ATC)

- `E` – in the training marker, open the training menu
- `F2` – cursor for the training cards

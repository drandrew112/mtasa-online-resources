# Job Creator
@keywords: job creator, editor, map, race, deathmatch, mission, stage, objective, freecam, F5, undo
@related: binds, binds_pause, binds_jobs

Start the Job Creator from the pause menu: **Online > Jobs > Creator**. Here you can build your own races, deathmatches and missions.

In a mission you place zones, enemy peds, vehicles, items and interaction points (**R > Place**), then build the stages in **R > Stages**: every stage has objectives (get to a zone, take an item, kill enemies ...) and may have fail conditions. **Mission Settings** sets the Team lives, the time limit, the players' weapons and the reward. **Test** runs the mission alone from any stage, without rewards.

## Keys

- `R` – the editor menu
- `F5` – switch between free camera and on foot
- Hold the right mouse button – look around in the free camera
- Left mouse button – place / select
- `Enter` – confirm
- `Backspace` – cancel placing or moving, clear the selection
- `Delete` – delete the selected item
- `Ctrl + Z` – undo
- `Ctrl + Y` – redo
- `Space` – take the photo (in photo mode)
- `F6` – stop the test

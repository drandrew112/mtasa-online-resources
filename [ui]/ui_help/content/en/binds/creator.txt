# Job Creator
@keywords: job creator, editor, map, race, deathmatch, freecam, F5, undo
@related: binds, binds_pause

Start the Job Creator from the pause menu: **Online > Jobs > Creator**. Here you can build your own race and deathmatch maps.

## Keys

- `R` – the editor menu
- `F5` – switch between free camera and on foot
- Hold the right mouse button – look around in the free camera
- Left mouse button – place / select
- `Enter` – confirm
- `Backspace` – cancel placing or moving, clear the selection
- `Delete` – delete the selected item
- `Ctrl + Z` – undo
- `Ctrl + Y` – redo
- `Space` – take the photo (in photo mode)
- `F6` – stop the test

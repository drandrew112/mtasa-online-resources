# Phone
@keywords: B, phone, contacts, call, ambulance, emergency, email, mail, messages, browser
@related: binds, binds_social, feature_phone, feature_browser

`B` takes out the phone. It is fully keyboard controlled.

## Keys

- `B` – take out / put away
- Arrows – move between the apps on the home screen
- `Up` `Down` arrow – move in a list
- `Enter` – open an app, select an item
- `Backspace` – back; on the home screen it puts the phone away
- `Esc` – put the phone away

## Apps

- **Contacts** – calls, for example the emergency services (ambulance) or the insurance.
- **Messages** – the messages of the social panel.
- **Email** – mails sent by the server.
- **Browser** – the in-game websites.
- **My vehicles**, **Organization**, **Invites**, **Settings**.

# Key binds – overview
@keywords: keys, binds, bind, keyboard, controls, hotkey, shortcut

These are the server's own keys. The standard GTA controls (walking, driving, shooting) are unchanged in the MTA settings and are not listed here. Every panel has its own page: click the links for the details.

> While most panels are open, the keys of the other panels do not work. Close the open one first (usually `Backspace` or `Esc`).

## The most important keys

- `F1` – this help panel
- `T` – write in the chat
- `P` – pause menu (map, Online, stats, settings)
- `M` – interaction menu
- `Home` (or `Num 7`) – social panel (friends, crew, messages)
- `B` – phone
- `I` – inventory
- `Tab` (hold) – weapon wheel
- `Y` – HUD info (money, level) for a few seconds
- `E` – use key in most markers
- `X` – 3D menu of nearby objects

## Introduction (first join)

- `Space` – continue to the next step (when the step allows it)
- `PgDn` – skip the current module
- `End` – skip the whole introduction

> If a key does nothing, check that no other panel is open and that you are not typing in the chat.

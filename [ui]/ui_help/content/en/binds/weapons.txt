# Weapons
@keywords: Tab, weapon, weapon wheel, selector, reload, R, clip
@related: binds, binds_vehicle

## Weapon wheel

- Hold `Tab` – open the wheel
- Move the mouse – choose a category
- Mouse wheel – switch weapon inside the category
- Release `Tab` – take the selected weapon

While the wheel is open you can keep walking, but you cannot shoot.

## Other

- `R` – reload (on foot, when the clip is not full and you have ammo left)

> You can buy weapons in the Ammu-Nation shops: press `E` in the shop marker.

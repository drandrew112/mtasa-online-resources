# Vehicles
@keywords: vehicle, car, headlights, L, radio, Q, siren, ELS, lights, horn, drive-by, exit
@related: binds, binds_interaction

## Basics

- `L` – headlights on / off (driver only)
- Hold `Q` – radio selector; switch station with the mouse wheel, release to close (not in planes and helicopters)

## Emergency lights and siren

In an emergency vehicle, as the driver:

- `0` – lights on / off
- `1` – siren on / off
- `2` – next siren tone
- Hold `3` – air horn
- `4` – secondary siren on / off (if the type has one)
- `5` – next flash pattern
- Short horn press – siren on, then another short press changes the tone
- Double horn tap – siren off
- Hold the horn – normal horn

Choose the siren type in the `M` menu under **Vehicle > Siren type**.

## Drive-by (as a passenger)

- Right mouse button – drive-by on / off
- `E` / `Q` – next / previous weapon during drive-by

## Garage (tuning)

- Move the mouse – turn the camera around the vehicle
- Mouse wheel – zoom in / out

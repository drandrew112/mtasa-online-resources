# Interaction menu (M)
@keywords: M, interaction, menu, fast travel, vehicle, personal vehicle, repair, collectibles, preferences, spawn
@related: binds, binds_vehicle, feature_interaction

The menu on `M` gives quick access to common things. Other systems use the same menu as a temporary menu too (for example in shops and editors).

## Keys

- `M` – open / close
- `Up` `Down` arrow – move
- `Left` `Right` arrow – change the value of a choice row
- `Enter` – select, open a submenu
- `Backspace` – one level back / close

## What can you do here?

- **Fast travel** – between the three airports (LS, LV, SF).
- **Vehicle** – request or restore your personal vehicle, repair, siren type.
- **Collectibles** – your stunt jumps and UFO parts.
- **Stats** – played time, level.
- **Preferences** – where you spawn when you join (last location / random).

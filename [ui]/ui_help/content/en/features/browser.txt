The game has its own "internet": websites where you buy vehicles, do your banking, register a company and see what is going on on the server. Open the browser with the **Browser** app of the phone. Use it with the mouse: the wheel scrolls, the button top left goes back, **X** top right closes it; the right mouse button also goes back. While it is open, your cash and bank balance show top right.

![300](content/images/features/browser_home.jpg)

## Open SE – the home page

The home page is a search engine. Type an address (for example `lvcars.eu`), a category or any words into the field. Below it are the categories (Entertainment, Finance, Business, Vehicles, Property, Services) and a few recommended sites.

## Buying a vehicle – lvcars.eu, legendary.eu

![300](content/images/features/browser_lvcars.jpg)

- **Las Venturas Cars** (`lvcars.eu`) – certified used cars with warranty, sortable by price.
- **Legendary Wheels** (`legendary.eu`) – hypercars from the most exclusive showroom in the state.

Pick a car and a colour, then buy it. Prices include VAT; you pay from your cash first and from your bank account if the cash is not enough. The car is registered to your account: summon it from the **My Vehicles** phone app or with **Request personal vehicle** in the interaction menu. Only owned cars can be tuned.

## Bank – libertybank.eu

![300](content/images/features/browser_bank.jpg)

**Liberty Bank** is your bank account: balance, deposit and withdraw cash, transfers to others, your statement, plus taking a personal loan and your running loans. Accounts of the state or a company that you have rights to show on their own tabs.

## Registering a company – sabusinessregistry.eu

On the **San Andreas Business Registry**:

- **Register** – register an organization (company): a name, a headquarters (office), then the payment from your bank account (registration fee + the price of the headquarters). One organization per person.
- **Registry** – search the registered companies.
- **Top organizations** – the most valuable companies.

After that you run your company on the office computer, under **My organization** in the interaction menu and in the **Organization** phone app.

## State transparency – gov.sa

The **State of San Andreas** site shows the state's finances publicly, anyone can look:

- **Overview** – the state at a glance, the treasury's daily income and expense by category.
- **Budget** – the budget of the state companies.
- **Taxes** – company and property tax, the personal income tax keys, VAT by product group.
- **Fees** – fees, fines and the state healthcare.
- **Public pay** – the state salaries.
- **Votes** – the state votes.

Every financial item is shown at most 30 days back.

## Ambulance service – ems-dispatch.eu

The **San Andreas EMS** site: an introduction, live (anonymous) figures of the rescues and a map of the hospitals. The **dispatcher console** and the **unit tablet** used by the paramedics and dispatchers on duty also open from here.

## Air traffic control – sanandreascontrol.eu

**San Andreas Control** is the air traffic control site: how to become a controller, the letters of agreement, the procedures, the airspace map, the live ATC status (which positions are staffed) and the ATC software manual.

## More sites

- `sa-transit.sa` – **San Andreas Transit**: journey planner, departures and the live map of the trains.
- `blood-transport.eu` – the blood transport service: live map, stock of the blood banks and hospitals, how to join.
- `diamond.eu` – the Diamond Casino, opening soon.

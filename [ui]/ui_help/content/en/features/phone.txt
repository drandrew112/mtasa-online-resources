# Phone
@keywords: B, phone, app, contacts, call, Julia, Markus, insurance, emergency, ambulance, EMS, messages, email, mail, browser, my vehicles, organization, associate, invites, lobby, wallpaper
@related: binds_phone, feature_browser, feature_social, feature_interaction

Take out the phone with `B`. It works with the keyboard: move with the arrows, open something with `Enter`, go back with `Backspace`.

![300](content/images/features/phone_home.jpg)

## Apps

- **Contacts** – people you can call, see below.
- **Messages** – the chats of the social panel (read only). Write in the social panel.
- **Email** – the server's mails, for example the weekly updates (the news of the week, weekly bonuses).
- **Browser** – opens the in-game browser (buying vehicles, bank, registering a company, the state's site…).
- **My Vehicles** – the vehicles you bought, by type (land, boat, helicopter, plane). Pick one to have it brought near you. You see which one is out and which one is destroyed.
- **Organization** – your company's people: invite players as associates (with their distance), remove associates; when someone invites you, accept or decline here, and leave here. Without a company, register one on sabusinessregistry.eu.
- **Invites** – lobby invitations you received (jobs); `Enter` joins.
- **Settings** – the phone wallpaper, even in your crew's colour.

## Contacts

Call a contact in **Contacts**; after a short ring you see what you can ask for:

![300](content/images/features/phone_contacts.jpg)

- **Julia** – heals you (full health) and refills your armour. Costs money (with VAT), paid in cash.
- **Markus** – gets you into **Arena War**. **Request Job** does not work yet; Markus will get back to you later.
- **Insurance** – when one of your own vehicles is destroyed, claim its repair here (one fee per vehicle with VAT; from your bank account first, cash if the bank is not enough). With no destroyed vehicle the call does not connect.
- **Emergency Services** – **Ambulance (EMS)**: give a short title and a description of the emergency, and the dispatch opens a case at your position; an ambulance crew on duty comes out. With nobody on duty the call does not go through. The police and fire department lines are not available yet.

# Social panel
@keywords: social, Home, Num 7, friend, friends, request, crew, message, chat, achievement, milestone, XP, profile, account, password, email, display name
@related: binds_social, feature_phone, feature_pause

The social panel is about the other players: friends, crews and messages. You also find your own profile and achievements here. Open it with `Home` or `Num 7`, use it with the mouse, close it with `Esc`.

![300](content/images/features/social_friends.jpg)

The top bar has four tabs: **HOME**, **ACHIEVEMENTS**, **SOCIAL** and, on the right edge next to your name, **PROFILE**.

## Home

The **weekly updates** on the left, the friends who are online right now on the right.

## Social – friends and crews

On the SOCIAL tab pick one of six sections on the left:

- **FRIENDS** – your friend list and who is online. Click a friend to open their profile, where you can message or remove them.
- **REQUESTS** – friend requests sent to you; accept or decline them here.
- **FIND PLAYER** – searches online and offline (registered) players by name. Send a request with **Add friend** on their profile.
- **MESSAGES** – your private chats and the crew chat. Start a new chat with the **Message** button of a profile or a friend.
- **MY CREW** – your crew: members and founder. The founder can customise it (name, tag, colour, description) and kick members; anyone can leave. Without a crew you can create one here (name 3–24 characters + a short tag).
- **FIND CREW** – the existing crews you can join. You can be in one crew at a time.

> Your crew tag also shows on your nametag, and the crew colour can be your phone wallpaper.

## Achievements

Achievements are the milestones you reach in the game, listed by category: General, Driving, Walking, Sailing, Flying, Work, Games, Business. Each one gives XP towards your level. You see how many you unlocked and when; some stay hidden until you earn them.

![300](content/images/features/social_achievements.jpg)

## Profile and account settings

The **PROFILE** tab is your public profile: this is how others see you (display name, level, played time, crew). **Account settings** open from here:

- **Display name** – the name others see in the panel, the chat and on your nametag.
- **Email address** – changing it needs your current password. The weekly update emails go to this address (turn them on in the pause menu, Settings / Online).
- **Change password** – current password, then the new one twice.
- The account name (the one you log in with) can't be changed.

> You can also read your messages in the Messages app of the phone.

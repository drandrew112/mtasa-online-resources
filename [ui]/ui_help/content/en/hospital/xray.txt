# X-ray
@keywords: x-ray, side, lead wall, control desk, cast, control image, viewer
@related: hospital_cast, hospital_ct

- 14 body parts; for limbs you pick the **side**. Wrong side = negative image and the test is not indicated.
- For the exposure you stand **at the control desk behind the lead wall** (3 s).
- The image also shows on the **wall viewer** (north wall).
- A **control image** can be taken in a cast.

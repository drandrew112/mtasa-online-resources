# Cast room
@keywords: cast, reduction, fracture, sling, circulation, x-ray
@related: hospital_xray, hospital_wound

1. **X-ray** (mandatory).
2. **Reduction** of a displaced fracture (minigame) – pain relief first.
3. **Cast** (minigame). A cast that is too tight must be redone.
4. Check **circulation, movement, sensation**; a **sling** for the arm.
5. **Control X-ray** in the cast.

# STEMI
@keywords: stemi, nstemi, acs, infarction, pci, lysis, nitrate, troponin
@related: hospital_ecg, hospital_surgery

- **12-lead ECG** at once; troponin in the cardiac lab.
- **Inferior STEMI:** ST elevation in II, III, aVF; risk of AV block; **no nitrate** (the BP falls).
- **Anterior STEMI:** V1–V4; risk of ventricular fibrillation.
- Treatment: aspirin, clopidogrel, heparin, then **primary PCI** (theatre, goes first) or lysis.
- The heart muscle dies until reperfusion: every minute counts.

# Transfusion
@keywords: transfusion, blood, blood group, crossmatch, 0 negative, ab plasma, massive
@related: hospital_lab, hospital_shock

- Without a blood group lab only **0 Rh− red cells** and **AB plasma** (from the shock room fridge).
- After the group a **crossmatch** (60 s), then group-specific units.
- **Red cells:** the donor's ABO antigens must be in the recipient, Rh+ only for Rh+. **Plasma:** the reverse (AB for everyone).
- One unit takes 5 minutes; a **massive transfusion** (4+ units in 10 minutes) is for physicians, with the pressure bag 2 minutes.
- Incompatible blood → **acute haemolytic reaction**. Too much, too fast → fluid overload.
- When the blood runs out, an emergency shipment arrives after 10 minutes.

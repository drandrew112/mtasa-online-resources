# Shock room
@keywords: shock room, rsi, intubation, drain, pelvic binder, ventilation, wall monitor
@related: hospital_fast, hospital_ecg, hospital_transfusion

In a shock bay the patient is **connected to the wall monitor by itself**. Only here (and in the ICU):

- RSI / intubation and the RSI drugs, noradrenaline, alteplase,
- needle decompression, **chest drain** (minigame), pelvic binder,
- ventilator, CPAP, arterial blood gas, FAST, 12-lead ECG.

> In cardiac arrest and in an unconscious patient the airway may be secured anywhere.

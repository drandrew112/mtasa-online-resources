# Orders and indications
@keywords: order, indication, points, limit, resident, nurse, drug order
@related: hospital_lab, hospital_ct, hospital_xray, hospital_pay

Orders are given by a resident or higher; carrying them out is allowed from the intervention's own rank.

- On the order list **✓** is indicated, **!** is an order without indication.
- **Without indication** only half of the points are given.
- Every code has a **per-case limit** (e.g. head CT 2×, lab panels 3×).
- **Drug order:** a nurse may give the ordered medicine; giving it closes the order.

> Indication examples: head injury with loss of consciousness or an anticoagulant → head CT; suspected fracture → X-ray before the cast.

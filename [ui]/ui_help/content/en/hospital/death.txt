# Death and the morgue
@keywords: dead, death, morgue, death certificate, icd, morgue staff
@related: hospital_docs, hospital_surgery

1. A **physician** records the death: the time, the **cause with an ICD code** (e.g. R9600, I4610), the circumstances.
2. The body stays on the trolley and can be pushed; take it to the **morgue drop point**.
3. At the drop the **death certificate** is written.

- Dead on arrival by ambulance: a "dead" case on the hospital trolley.
- Nobody takes it to the morgue for 20 minutes: the NPC morgue staff takes it, no points.

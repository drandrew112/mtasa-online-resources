# Poisoning
@keywords: poisoning, antidote, tox, paracetamol, organophosphate, co, cyanide, methanol
@related: hospital_lab

The **tox lab** (or the full lab) finds the substance.

- **Paracetamol:** N-acetylcysteine.
- **TCA:** wide QRS → sodium bicarbonate.
- **Organophosphate:** atropine + pralidoxime (low cholinesterase).
- **Carbon monoxide:** 100% oxygen (high COHb).
- **Beta blocker:** glucagon. **Digoxin:** digoxin Fab.
- **Methanol:** fomepizole. **Cyanide:** hydroxocobalamin.
- **Opioid:** naloxone. **Benzodiazepine:** flumazenil (carefully).

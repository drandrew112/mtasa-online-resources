# Triage and life-saving care
@keywords: triage, life saving, K10, urgency, triage helper
@related: hospital_pathway, hospital_panel

## Levels

- **1 – immediate:** cardiac arrest, airway obstruction, severe shock.
- **2 – urgent:** unstable values, severe pain, suspicious chest pain.
- **3 – deferrable:** stable but needs care.
- **4 – non-urgent:** minor injury.

## Before triage (K10)

Before triage only **life-saving** care is allowed: CPR, airway, bleeding control, oxygen, monitor and examinations. In cardiac arrest everything.

> The **triage helper** in the right column of the patient panel suggests a level from the measured values. You decide.

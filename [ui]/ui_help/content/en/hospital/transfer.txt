# Transfer (S1/S2)
@keywords: transfer, s1, s2, transport, als, patient pickup, erm
@related: hospital_pathway, hospital_docs

- **S1:** immediate (a physician asks). **S2:** within 30 minutes (from resident).
- Needs: oxygen, syringe pump, ventilation, monitor → only an **ALS / DOC / HELI** unit.
- Target hospital and a **note**: the target's handover text is made from it.
- **A suitable unit is signed in:** an ERM task (S1 purple, S2 light purple badge). The crew pushes its empty trolley next to the hospital one at the **"Patient pickup"** marker (5 s).
- **No unit:** an automatic transfer after 3 minutes (S1) or 10 minutes (S2).

# Patient panel
@keywords: patient panel, examination, body map, tabs, status
@related: hospital_orders, hospital_docs, hospital_shock

Opened with **Examine patient** in the patient's menu (at the bedside) or from a patient row on the PC / tablet.

- **Header:** triage, name, age, sex, weight, case number, treating doctor, place, blood group, allergy.
- **Body map:** findings and treated parts by colour.
- **Vital signs bar:** only from the monitor or a measurement.
- **Tabs:** Examination (10 body regions, history, GCS / NIHSS / NRS), Orders, Procedures, Drugs, Transfusion, Documentation (PC only), Disposition (outcome: discharge, ward, ICU, theatre, transfer, death), Pathway.
- **Close:** the **X** button at the top right (or `ESC`).
- **Result:** clicking an order shows only the result; **X** brings the panel back.

> History, status, epicrisis and BNO diagnoses can be recorded **only at a hospital computer**, not on the tablet.

> From a distance (PC, tablet) the physical exam and the procedures are blocked: stand next to the patient.

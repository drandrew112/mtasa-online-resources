# Patient pathway
@keywords: pathway, arrival, ambulance, walk-in, register, treating doctor, outcome
@related: hospital_triage, hospital_panel, hospital_docs

1. **Arrival:** by ambulance (onto a corridor spot F1–F5), on foot (waiting room) or carried in ("Register" in the patient's menu inside the ER zone).
2. **Triage** (1–4) – before it only life-saving care (see Triage).
3. **Treating doctor:** a resident or higher takes the patient.
4. **Examination and orders:** patient panel, lab, imaging, ECG.
5. **Treatment:** in the rooms (shock room, wound room, cast room).
6. **Diagnosis** (ICD, at most 5).
7. **Outcome:** home, ward, ICU, theatre, transfer (S1/S2) or death. At closing the **Ambulatory sheet** is written.

> Every step goes into the pathway (Pathway tab) with the time and who did it.

# 12-lead ECG
@keywords: ecg, 12 lead, stemi, electrode, rhythm, eMedia
@related: hospital_stemi, hospital_shock

The 12-lead ECG is taken in the **shock room** (the ECG machine is there); in an ALS ambulance from B+ competence too.

- Drag the electrodes from the tray onto the patient. As a **nurse or resident** their spots are labelled, and the spot of the electrode in your hand is highlighted.
- Swapped arm electrodes: negative lead I.
- A wrong chest electrode: a distorted R progression.
- The report gives the machine reading, the measured values (PR, QRS, QT, axis) and the 12 leads.

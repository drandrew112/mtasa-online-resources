# Lab and blood draw
@keywords: lab, blood draw, tube order, haemolysis, EDTA, citrate, full lab
@related: hospital_orders, hospital_transfusion

Order by panel (basic, trauma, cardiac, clotting, blood gas, tox, inflammation, blood group, crossmatch) or the **full lab** (everything at once). The result comes in about **2 minutes**.

## Blood draw (minigame)

The right tube order: **citrate → serum → heparin → EDTA → fluoride**.

- **Haemolysis:** false high potassium.
- **EDTA before serum:** false high potassium, false low calcium.
- **Underfilled citrate:** falsely long clotting.
- **Infusion arm:** diluted sample.

> The "What do the abnormal values mean?" box of the lab report explains every abnormal value.

# Wound room
@keywords: wound, suture, irrigation, disinfection, local anaesthesia, tetanus, dressing
@related: hospital_cast, hospital_orders

1. **Irrigation** (minigame) – wash a dirty wound thoroughly.
2. **Local anaesthesia** – without it the patient moves and the suture is worse.
3. **Suture** (minigame).
4. **Disinfection**, **final dressing**. For burns a burn dressing.

Tetanus prophylaxis: when the last shot was over 10 years ago or is unknown. Poor care means a risk of infection.

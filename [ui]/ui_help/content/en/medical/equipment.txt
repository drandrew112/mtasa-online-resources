Every ambulance, doctor car (the Skoda Octavia) and rescue helicopter (Maverick) carries one **medical bag** and one **Lifepak 15** monitor / defibrillator. Only medics on duty can use them.

## Taking them out

Stand at the side door of the ambulance (the boot of the doctor car, the right rear door of the helicopter) and press `X` on the **Ambulance equipment** 3D menu. Pick the line with its number key:

1. **Take medical bag**
2. **Take monitor / defibrillator**
3. **Take both**
4. **Put medical bag back**
5. **Put monitor back**
6. **Put everything back**
7. **Restock bag**
8. **Check contents** – what is left in the bag (view only).

The items go into your hands. `H` puts them down on the ground. When a procedure starts, carried items are put down next to the patient automatically. A dropped item is picked up again from its own 3D menu. Each ambulance takes back only its own items.

## What the panel needs

The items must be within **4 metres** of the patient to start a procedure (6 metres keeps a running one connected, for example the oxygen).

- **Medical bag:** Bandage, Splint, IV access, Intubate, O2 mask, Medication, Glucometer.
- **Lifepak:** the monitor and the defibrillator.
- **Nothing:** Neuro exam, CPR, Request transport.

## What is in a full bag

- **IV kits:** 6 – one is used up by every IV attempt (also a failed one).
- **Bandages:** 6 – one per successful dressing.
- **Splints:** 2 – one per successful splint.
- **Oxygen cylinder:** 100% – used all the time while a patient has the O2 mask on or is intubated, also on the stretcher, in the ambulance and at the handover. A full cylinder lasts **15 minutes**. Warning at 20%.
- **Glucometer**, **airway kit** – needed, not used up.
- **Medicines:** Adrenalin 5, Fentanyl 3, Captopril 3, Nitroglycerin 3, Ketamine 2, Rocuronium 2, Glucose 40% 2, Oral glucose gel 2, Naloxone 2, Salbutamol 2, Midazolam 2, Insulin 1.

The panel shows the stock: the xN on the medicine cards, the number on the **IV access** button and the **O2 %** chip. A warning comes when something drops to a quarter. When something runs out, the button says **out of stock – restock the bag at a hospital**.

## Restocking

1. Put the bag back into the ambulance (**Put medical bag back**).
2. Drive to a hospital and park in a yellow **Ambulance Bay** marker.
3. At the side door: `X`, then `7` – **Restock bag**.
4. Wait 6 seconds. Everything is refilled, the oxygen too. It is free.

While the ambulance stands in a bay with items missing, **EQUIPMENT MISSING** is shown above the side door.

## Do not leave anything behind

When the bag or the Lifepak lies on the ground 60 metres from its ambulance (20 metres while the ambulance drives away) and no medic is near it, you get a warning and a red blip on the map. After **10 minutes** it returns to the ambulance by itself, but **empty**: the medicines, IV kits, bandages and splints are gone and the cylinder is empty. Restock it.

> On the stretcher: the carried items ride on the side of the stretcher while you push it, and go into the ambulance with the patient.

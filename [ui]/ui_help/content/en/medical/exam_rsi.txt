# RSI exam
@keywords: RSI, rapid sequence intubation, intubation, preoxygenation, Ketamine, Rocuronium, tube, GCS, airway
@related: medical_exams, medical_procedures, medical_drugs

From B+. Required for the paramedic officer exam.

## When to intubate

- The patient cannot protect the airway: GCS ≤ 8, snoring or gurgling breathing in an unconscious patient.
- The oxygenation does not improve even with oxygen.
- Airway burn (sooty mouth, hoarse voice, stridor): early, because the airway swells fast.

## The order

1. **Preoxygenation:** about 3 minutes of oxygen on the mask to build a reserve.
2. **Induction:** Ketamine 1.5 mg/kg. Keeps the blood pressure, safe in shock.
3. **Paralysis:** Rocuronium 1–1.2 mg/kg. The patient soon stops breathing.
4. **Intubation.**
5. **Ventilation with oxygen** through the tube, monitoring, transport.

- Rocuronium works for long (40–60 minutes): the patient needs ventilation all the time.
- Without oxygen the tube does not help: when it runs out, change the oxygen source at once.
- Failed intubation and falling SpO2: stop, oxygenate on the mask, then try again.
- Fentanyl is optional: a higher dose dulls the stress response but lowers the breathing and the pressure.

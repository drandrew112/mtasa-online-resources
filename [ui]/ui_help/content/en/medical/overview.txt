# Medical system: overview
@keywords: medical, medic, EMS, ambulance, patient, examine, ABCDE, clinical death, cardiac arrest, consciousness, vitals, death timer, hospital, medsys
@related: medical_exam, medical_diagnosis, medical_monitor, medical_injuries, medical_drugs, medical_procedures, medical_equipment, medical_cases, binds_work

Patients are injured or sick NPCs: crash victims, people who were shot, overdoses, heart attacks, strokes. Players never get a medical state. Only players on **EMS duty** (the medic role, see the EMS work) can examine and treat them.

## How to start

1. Walk up to the patient, press `X` on the **Patient** 3D menu and pick **Examine patient**. The examination panel opens.
2. Go through the patient from the top: **A**irway, **B**reathing, **C**irculation, **D**isability (the ABCDE approach). The buttons of the panel are grouped the same way.
3. Fix what kills first: no pulse, then breathing / oxygen, then bleeding, then the rest.
4. Stabilise the patient, then bring them to a hospital on the stretcher (or request transport).

> Most interventions need the **medical bag** and the **Lifepak** (monitor / defibrillator) next to the patient. Take them out of the ambulance first: [[medical_equipment|Ambulance equipment]].

## Vital signs

A healthy adult has a pulse of about **72 / min**, a blood pressure of **120/80 mmHg**, an oxygen saturation (**SpO2**) of **98%**, **5 litres** of blood and a blood glucose of about **95 mg/dL**. Every value moves towards a target that the injuries, conditions and medicines set, so changes take a few seconds, not one instant.

- The pulse always rises as the blood pressure falls (the body tries to compensate). A racing pulse with a low pressure is shock.
- Pain above 7/10, an SpO2 below 88% or a systolic pressure below 90 makes the patient **dazed**.
- An SpO2 below 70% or a systolic pressure below 65 makes the patient **unconscious**.

## Consciousness

From best to worst:

- **Stable** – awake, oriented.
- **Confused** – awake but disoriented (intoxication, low / high sugar, stroke, after a seizure).
- **Dazed** – drowsy, only responds to voice.
- **Unconscious** – responds to pain only.
- **Clinical death** – no pulse. The panel shows a **Time left** counter.
- **Dead** – biological death. Only **Request transport** (hearse) is left.

## Cardiac arrest and the death timer

The heart stops when:

- the SpO2 falls to 0%,
- half of the blood is lost (2.5 litres),
- the systolic pressure falls to 30 mmHg (for example a wrongly given blood pressure medicine),
- the pulse stays at 200 or above for 5 seconds,
- the blood glucose stays under 20 mg/dL for 4 minutes,
- an electric shock hits a beating heart.

From then on you have **5 minutes**. Every good CPR round adds **45 seconds** to the counter. When it runs out, the patient is dead.

>! A patient who lost too much blood cannot get a pulse back however well you do CPR. Get IV access and run fluids during the resuscitation.

## Several medics on one patient

More medics can work on the same patient at the same time, each on a different thing: one bandages, the other places the IV, a third does CPR. Two medics never treat the same wound: the injury row shows **"name is treating it"** and the button is disabled for the others.

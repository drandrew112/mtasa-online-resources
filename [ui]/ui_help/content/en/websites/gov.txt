# State of San Andreas – gov.sa
@keywords: state, government, gov.sa, State of San Andreas, open government, transparency, treasury, budget, tax, taxes, income tax, VAT, property tax, company tax, fees, fines, healthcare, public pay, wages, salary, vote, votes
@related: web, web_bank, web_registry

**gov.sa** is the official website of the State of San Andreas. For transparency the state's operation is public: anyone can see where the money comes from and where it goes. The pages are on the menu at the top.

## Overview

The state at a glance, the **treasury's daily income and expense** on a chart and the treasury broken down **by category**.

## Budget

The income and expense of every **state company**. The wages of the public works a company pays are listed under it.

## Taxes

- **Company and property tax**.
- **Personal income tax keys** – which key applies to which income.
- **VAT by product group** – VAT is added on top of the shop price and goes to the State Treasury.

## Fees

The **fees and fines** of the state and the **state healthcare** (what medical care costs).

## Public pay

Who pays each work, its **wage multiplier** and its income tax key.

## Votes

The state votes. Citizens vote in game with `/vote`; the results are published when a vote closes.

> Money data is shown for the last 30 days only.

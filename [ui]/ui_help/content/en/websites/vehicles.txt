# Vehicle dealers – lvcars.eu, legendary.eu
@keywords: buy vehicle, buy car, car dealer, dealership, lvcars, Las Venturas Cars, legendary, Legendary Wheels, hypercar, used car, personal vehicle, VAT
@related: web, feature_browser, feature_phone

Personal cars are bought on two websites of the **Vehicles** category. Both work the same way, only the offer differs.

![300](content/images/features/browser_lvcars.jpg)

- **Las Venturas Cars** (`lvcars.eu`) – certified used cars with warranty, from city runabouts to SUVs and rally coupes. The list can be sorted by price.
- **Legendary Wheels** (`legendary.eu`) – hypercars from the most exclusive showroom in the state.

## Buying a car

1. Open the site and pick a car from the list.
2. Choose its colour.
3. Press buy. The price already includes VAT.

You pay from your cash first; if that is not enough, the rest comes from your bank account. The car is registered to your game account.

## After the purchase

- Summon the car with the **My Vehicles** phone app or with **Request personal vehicle** in the interaction menu.
- Only owned cars can be tuned in a workshop.

> The VAT of every purchase goes to the State Treasury; you can follow it on [[web_gov|gov.sa]].

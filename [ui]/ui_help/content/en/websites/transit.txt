**San Andreas Transit** is the public transport website: trains now, buses soon. The pages are on the menu at the top.

## Journey planner

Plan a trip on public transport. Pick the **From** and **To** stops (or click a stop on the map), choose **Now** or a departure time, then **Search journeys**. The results use live times and include the changes between lines.

## Departures

The live departure and arrival boards of every stop – the same as on the station displays.

## Network

The lines of the network and their timetables.

## Live map

Every train on the network, live: where it is, its next stops, its timetable and its delay. The stations show their departures and arrivals as well.

## Work with us

The works connected to public transport. **Sunline Rail** is looking for train drivers: drive regional and intercity services between Los Santos, San Fierro and Las Venturas, get paid for every service and climb the ranks. The page tells you how to start.

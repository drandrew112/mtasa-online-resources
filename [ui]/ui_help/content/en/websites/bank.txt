# Liberty Bank – libertybank.eu
@keywords: bank, Liberty Bank, libertybank, bank account, balance, deposit, withdraw, cash, transfer, statement, transactions, account number, loan, company account
@related: web, web_registry, feature_browser

**Liberty Bank** is the bank of San Andreas. On its website you manage every bank account you have access to: your own personal account, and the company or state accounts you were given rights to.

![300](content/images/features/browser_bank.jpg)

## Accounts

The buttons at the top switch between your accounts: **Personal** is your own, the others are the company and state accounts you have rights to. Each one shows its type, account number (for example `LB-1-000042`) and balance. A frozen or closed account is marked in red.

## Deposit and withdraw

On your **personal** account you can pay cash in (**Deposit cash**) and take cash out (**Withdraw cash**): pick an amount or **All**. Next to it you see the cash on hand.

## Transfer

1. **Recipient** – the account number of the recipient.
2. **Amount** – how much to send.
3. **Note** – optional message for the recipient.
4. **Check** – the bank shows who receives the money; **Confirm transfer** sends it, **Cancel** drops it.

From a company or state account you can only send money if your rights allow it.

## Statement

The **Statement** lists the transactions of the selected account: incoming and outgoing money, with the date and the note.

## Loans

Under **Loans** you see your running loans and can take a new personal loan. On a company account you see the loans of the company instead (if you have the right).

# The console and the scope
@keywords: ATC console, login, log in, position list, scope, radar, top bar, map, flight list, zoom, speed vector, display buttons, AT, FIXL, refresh, Hide, Logout, reset view
@related: atc, atc_labels, atc_commands, web_atc

The controller software of San Andreas Control: how to log in and what the radar scope shows. (SACC-SW-1, version 1.0.)

## 1. Logging in

1. Go to an **ATC console**: a marker in the San Andreas Control building in San Fierro. Every position is worked from there; the airport control towers have no consoles.
2. Press `E`. The position list opens.
3. Pick a position and press **Log in**.

![340](content/images/atc/login.jpg)

The position list. Each console offers only its own positions. A position that someone else already works shows "Staffed" and their name.

- You only see the positions you have the right for (TWR, APP, RADAR).
- Each console offers only its own positions:
  - **ACC** – SACC_CTR, SACC_N_CTR, SACC_S_CTR, SACC_U_CTR, SACC_NU_CTR, SACC_SU_CTR
  - **Los Santos** – SALS_TWR, SALS_APP
  - **San Fierro** – SASF_TWR, SASF_APP
  - **Las Venturas** – SALV_TWR, SALV_APP
- Logging in to another position logs you out of the old one.

The scope opens straight away. **Hide** in the top bar (or `E` at the console again) hides it, but you stay logged in. **Logout** ends your shift and pays you.

## 2. The scope

![380](content/images/atc/scope-centre.jpg)

San Andreas Control (SACC_CTR) with the whole FIR: the CTA, the three TMAs (solid) and CTRs (dashed), the fixes, the traffic and the flight lists on the right.

The scope has four parts:

- **Top bar** – your position and its name, your sectors (Control only, `SECT NL SL`), the runways in use (`SALS ARR 27L DEP 27R`), the wind, the display buttons, the UTC time, and the List / Reset view / Hide / Logout buttons.
- **Map** – airspaces, airports, fixes, the traffic with labels.
- **Flight lists** – on the right (List: on / off).
- **Bottom left** – the scale bar (in nm) and the world position under the cursor.

### Default view of each position

- **Control** – the whole FIR.
- **Approach** – its TMA.
- **Tower** – the airfield: runways, taxiways, stands.

This is the view after login and after **Reset view**.

![380](content/images/atc/scope-approach.jpg)

Los Santos Approach: the TMA, the CTR (dashed), the procedure points and an arrival on SANTA1A 27L.

![380](content/images/atc/scope-tower.jpg)

Los Santos Tower: runways, taxiways, stands and the ground labels. LCX6979 and VCA502 wait for their IFR clearance (yellow frame, "REQ IFR" in the list).

### Mouse and keys

- **Mouse wheel** – zoom at the cursor.
- **Right drag** – move the map.
- **Left click** on a label or a target – open the command menu.
- **Left drag** on a label – move the label (the leader line follows).
- **Right click** on a label – show / hide the route of the aircraft.
- **Right click** on an empty spot – hide every shown route.
- **Click** a row of a flight list – centre the map on that aircraft.
- `0` - `5` (also numpad) – speed vector length: 0 = off, 1-5 = 0.1-0.5 nm.

### Display buttons

The buttons in the middle of the top bar (hover over them for help):

- **AT** – airspace names and limits on / off.
- **FIXL** – fix names on / off (the fixes of a shown route stay named).
- **V-** / **V+** – shorter / longer speed vector.

### Radar refresh

The targets move only when the radar picture is refreshed: Tower every **1 s**, Approach every **2 s**, Control every **3 s**. Between refreshes they stand still.

### What each position sees

- **Tower, Approach** – the arrivals and departures of their airport, every aircraft they control, and (Approach) everything inside or crossing its TMA. Airborne traffic only up to **7500 ft**.
- **Control, lower or master** – everything.
- **Control, upper only** (U, NU, SU) – traffic from **FL185** up, plus their own aircraft.

The flight lists and transfers are on [[atc_commands|The command menu]].

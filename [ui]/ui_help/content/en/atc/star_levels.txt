# STAR entry levels
@keywords: STAR, entry level, entry fix, track, short STAR, highest entry, LOA entry, descent, VINEW1A, GROVE1A, PLAYA1A, OCEAN1A, MARLN1A, REEFS1A, SANTA1A, MISTY1A, ANGPI1A, DOHER1A, FLINT1A, GARCI1A, BAYSD1A, GANTN1A, JUNIP1A, PALIS1A, SHADY1A, MONTG1A, SPRNG1A, KACCY1A, DESRT1A, PRCKL1A, BONEC1A, GARCI2A
@related: atc_approach, atc_loa, atc_area, atc_nav

Annex A of the Approach manual. *Track* is the length of the STAR from the entry fix to the final fix. *Highest entry* is the highest level at the entry fix from which **every aircraft type** still reaches 1500 ft by the final fix on the STAR alone, cleared to 1500 ft at the entry fix (measured in the traffic simulation; the PC-12 is usually the slowest). *LOA entry* is the agreed level ([[atc_loa|Letter of Agreement]] section 2).

>! Where the LOA entry is above the highest entry, make the track longer with headings.

**(short)** marks the STARs where the LOA entry is 3000 ft.

How do I become an air traffic controller?

1. Go on duty

  1. Go to the San Andreas Control building in San Fierro. Every position is worked from here; the airport control towers have no consoles.
  2. Step into the duty marker at the entrance and go on duty in the Air traffic control work.
  3. Go inside, walk to the ATC console of your airport (or the ACC console) and press E. Pick a position and log in.
  4. Logout ends your shift and pays you for the traffic you worked. Separation losses in your sector are taken off your pay.

2. Your career

Everybody starts as a tower controller. Every accepted clearance gives work XP, and promotion opens the next kind of position.

TWR
Tower controllerfrom the start (Trainee, level 1)
APP
Approach controllerfrom level 5
RADAR
Radar controller (San Andreas Control)from level 8

A higher rank keeps the lower positions. Each position has a training module at the training marker in the operations room (ADC tower, APC approach, ACC radar); it opens together with the position. Your first shift offers ADC.

3. Read the documents in this order

  1. Airspace and navigationthe airspace, the points and the procedures
  2. ATC software manuallogging in, the scope, the commands
  3. Operations ManualsPart 1 general, then the part of your position
  4. Letter of Agreementwhere and how you hand aircraft over

To start, you only need Part 2 - Aerodrome control and a few sections of the Letter of Agreement (see Tower below).

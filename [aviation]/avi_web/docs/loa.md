# Letter of Agreement - transfer of control

**Document:** SACC-LOA-01 · **Version:** 1.1 · **Effective:** 12 October 2026

**Parties:** San Andreas Control (SACC) · Los Santos, San Fierro and Las Venturas Approach · Los Santos,
San Fierro and Las Venturas Tower

This agreement says where, when and in what condition the units of the San Andreas FIR hand
aircraft over to each other. Everything else (airspace, separation, how to work a position) is in
the [ATC Operations Manual](doc:ops-general). When two units agree something else on the spot, that
agreement applies to that one aircraft only.

---

## 1. General

1. Transfer every aircraft with **Transfer to** *before* it reaches the boundary of your airspace.
2. At the moment of transfer, the aircraft must meet the conditions of this agreement: the agreed
   level and the agreed procedure.
3. The receiving unit may change the level within its own airspace right after the transfer. Turns
   off the route are allowed only once the aircraft is inside the receiving unit's airspace, unless
   the units coordinated them.
4. When the receiving unit is closed, the aircraft continues uncontrolled. Before it leaves your
   airspace, leave it on its procedure or flight plan, at the level of this agreement.

## 2. San Andreas Control → Approach (arrivals)

| Item | Agreement |
| --- | --- |
| Procedure | The arrival has its **arrival procedure** (STAR + runway in use) |
| Transfer point | Before the **TMA entry fix** (the first fix of the STAR) |
| Level at the entry fix | **4000 ft or below**. **3000 ft or below** on the *short STARs* (Approach Operations, Annex A) |
| After the transfer | Approach may descend it straight away |

## 3. Approach → San Andreas Control (departures)

| Item | Agreement |
| --- | --- |
| Procedure | On its SID |
| Transfer point | Before the **SID exit fix** (the last fix of the SID, on the TMA boundary) |
| Level at the exit fix | **5000 ft**, climbing allowed only after the transfer |
| After the transfer | Control climbs it to its requested level |

SID exit fixes and STAR entry fixes often have the same name (for example VINEW1D and VINEW1A both
use VINEW). Departures at 5000 ft and arrivals at 4000 ft or below are 1000 ft apart there.

## 4. San Andreas Control ↔ Approach (overflights)

- Overflights cross a TMA **at or above 6000 ft**. They stay with Control and are not transferred.
- An overflight that has to cross a TMA below 6000 ft is coordinated with that Approach and
  transferred to it before the TMA boundary. Approach transfers it back before it leaves the TMA.

## 5. Approach → Tower (arrivals)

| Item | Agreement |
| --- | --- |
| Clearance | Approach has given **Cleared approach** |
| Transfer point | No later than the **final fix** |
| Level at the final fix | **1500 ft** cleared (at most 1700 ft above the field) |

## 6. Tower → Approach (departures)

| Item | Agreement |
| --- | --- |
| Clearance | IFR clearance with a SID and an initial level of **3000 ft**. Other initial levels, up to 5000 ft, only after coordination with Approach |
| Transfer point | **Once airborne**, no later than when it leaves the CTR (2000 ft) |

## 7. Tower → Approach (go-arounds)

| Item | Agreement |
| --- | --- |
| Procedure | The go-around procedure of the runway (`<ICAO>GA`), climbing to **3000 ft** |
| Transfer point | **As soon as it climbs out of the CTR** |
| Approach | Keeps 3000 ft free near the airport. Gives a lower level and a new **Cleared approach** |

## 8. San Fierro Approach ↔ Las Venturas Approach

The two TMAs share the fix **GARCI**. Flights between the two airports go straight from one Approach
to the other.

| Direction | Level at GARCI | Procedure after GARCI | Transfer point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| San Fierro → Las Venturas | **3000 ft** or below | GARCI2A | before GARCI |
| Las Venturas → San Fierro | **4000 ft** | GARCI1A | before GARCI |

## 9. Inside San Andreas Control

| Boundary | Agreement |
| --- | --- |
| North / South line (ZEPHR - PALIS - VINEW - east edge) | Transfer before the line |
| Lower → upper (FL205) | An aircraft cleared above FL205 is transferred before it reaches FL205 |
| Upper → lower (FL205) | A descending aircraft is transferred before it leaves FL205 |

When only SACC_CTR is open, it has the whole CTA, and none of these transfers are needed.
